# Ban Prevention

- Progress the game legitly (No 1 hit kill, god mode, speed multipliers, etc)

## What's okay to use?

- Hero summons
- Hero advancement
- Freezing certain special tab resources
  - Essentially freeze luxury resources for unlimited usage, so having 1 = unlimited

## What not to do?

- Don't hop into PvP and get reported
- Don't max paragons on new accounts

Bans usually happen after 4 days-- if your account survives 5-6 days, you're in the safe zone.
