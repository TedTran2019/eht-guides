# Unified Progression Guide (Updated Sept 15, 2026)

The goal of this guide is to put all the necessary information in one place plus include information
I wish I knew. In addition, to try to make matters as curt as possible for easy quick reference.

## Game Knowledge Tl;DR

### Hunters

Rarity goes from: normal, rare, superior, heroic (10-13), legendary (14-18), then ultimate (22+).
Heroic and above hunters get increasing buffs based on rarity.
For each stat line on the hero, it goes like:

- White (+0)
- Blue (+1)
- Orange (+2)

For all intents and purposes, heroic (10+ points) and above is all that matters.

#### Who do I keep? Minimum criteria

- Heroics+
- 13 point heroics with perfect characteristic (mos, heroic, charismatic) and orange attack speed line, legendaries+
  - For these perfect heroics,you can awaken their attack speed to purple and make them legendary. If you need to banish them because someone better comes along, you get an awakening crystal back anyway.

- Perfect heroics, legendaries with good characteristics, non-berserker legendaries w/ any characteristics, 18 stat legendaries
  - Non-aggroing legendaries can be used as loot goblins (100% chance of 2x gold, 100% chance of extra materials, then in the future max high class material drop chance)
    - Beserkers always aggro and can't be used, Dark Knights should avoid shadow lancer
    - Paladins can be changed to dark weapon and not aggro

- Legendaries+ with perfect characteristics and 18 stat legendaries (for rerolling characteristics)

Tip: Do not rush in making anyone ultimate-- giving your carries purple attack speed is the absolutely highest ROI
It's most likely more reliable to make a perfect heroic into an ultimate than it is to reroll a bad characteristic into an ideal one on a 18 stat legendary hunter.
By the time you reach end game, most people will use 6 awakening crystals to purple HP + all right-side lines.
If your heroic already has the majority of those stats orange, it'll take them 9 awakening crystals to go ultimate.
**Do NOT work towards this because this is just end game optimization.**
