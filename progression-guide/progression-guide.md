# Unified Progression Guide (Updated Sept 15, 2026)

The goal of this guide is to put all the necessary information in one place plus include information
I wish I knew. In addition, to try to make matters as curt as possible for easy quick reference.

## Game Knowledge Tl;DR

### Hunters

Rarity goes from: normal, rare, superior, heroic (10-13), legendary (14-18), then ultimate (22+).
Heroic and above hunters get increasing buffs based on rarity.
For each stat line on the hero, it goes like:

- White (+0)
- Blue (+1)
- Orange (+2)

For all intents and purposes, heroic (10+ points) and above is all that matters.

#### What are perfect and good characteristics?

Perfect characteristics:

- Charismatic on one ranger OR barbarian (You only need ONE)
- Man of Steel on sorcerer
- Heroic on ranger
- Man of Steel or Heroic on dark knight, paladin, berserker
  - Man of Steel > Heroic, though.

Good characteristics are typically:

- Swift > Strong > Sharp
- Sharp is technically better until you can max out crit chance without it, e.g high index completion.

#### Who do I keep? Minimum criteria

- Heroics+
- 13 point heroics with perfect characteristic (mos, heroic, charismatic) and orange attack speed line, legendaries+
  - For these perfect heroics, you can awaken their attack speed to purple and make them legendary. If you need to banish them because someone better comes along, you get an awakening crystal back anyway.

- Perfect heroics, legendaries with good characteristics, non-berserker legendaries w/ any characteristics, 18 stat legendaries
  - Non-aggroing legendaries can be used as loot goblins (100% chance of 2x gold, 100% chance of extra materials, then in the future high class material drop chance)
    - Beserkers always aggro and can't be used, Dark Knights should avoid shadow lancer
    - Paladins can be changed to dark weapon and not aggro

- Legendaries+ with perfect characteristics and 18 stat legendaries (for rerolling characteristics)

Tips:

1. Do not rush in making anyone ultimate-- giving your carries purple attack speed is the absolutely highest ROI.
2. It's most likely more reliable to make a perfect heroic into an ultimate than it is to reroll a bad characteristic into an ideal one on a 18 stat legendary hunter. By the time you reach end game, most people will use 6 awakening crystals to purple HP + all right-side lines. If your heroic already has the majority of those stats orange, it'll take them 9 awakening crystals to go ultimate.
**Do NOT work towards this because this is just end game optimization.**
3. Keep your roster as lean as possible for maximum hunter throughput and to reduce the amount of characters you have to gear

#### Should I reroll?

**Absolutely, yes. But ONLY if there are coupon codes to get you 450 gems so you can instantly reroll.**
While most guides will recommend two good paladins or one good sorc + paladin, I disagree with this.
The only main purpose of a paladin is to carry you to boost 30, and having him be legendary with a good
characteristic isn't a major boon over having him be a random characteristic heroic. You will get hundreds of heroics
in your first month of playing, so don't worry about it.

Your main true roadblocks in the game will be two things:

1. Creating a Blizzard Dark Lord (Sorcerer) that can survive Chaos 1 field
2. Defeating corrupted ranch and Chaos 1 field boss (Cx1 FB)

So I would say your one and ONLY priority is a legendary man of steel sorcerer. Not only will this make
creating a dark lord that can survive chaos 1 field much easier, but he'll also be someone that you use
for the entire game, so you don't have to worry about investing awakening crystals into him. You don't
want to be in a situation where you don't have a single hero worth investing awakening crystals into.
If you're not a masochist, I'd argue this would be the "reasonable" tier list

1. Legendary Man of Steel Sorcerer
2. Legendary perfect characteristic (NOT dark knight or berserker)
3. 18 stat legendary (NOT dark knight or berserker)
4. Legendary (NOT dark knight or berserker)
5. Any legendary

### I want to drop real world dough on this game, what's worth it?

#### Passes

- For VIP, aka daily bonuses on a monthly pass that are worth it:
  - Dungeon and Hunting Perks is only $3.49
    - Auto-dungeon makes token events easy, gives you unlimited phoenix feathers, and is the main way to get helmets
    - Field boss gives 420 relic tokens a month, this doubles it. If 20 relic tokens are valued at 100 gems, this is valued at 2100 gems. This essentially speeds up your relic progression by 11% late game (if you're using all challenger tokens and BOV medals on relics).
  - Ad skip is $7.69 and saves you 4 hours of your life a month.
- Everything else among VIP that is not worth it:
  - Gem and EXP perk: 900 gems a month + 50% extra materials (not HCM)
  - Equipment and Rune Storage perks: 200 extra equipment slots, 50 extra rune slots
  - Hunter invitation perk: Expected 1.2 legendaries and 5.4 heroics over course of a month.
  - Battle Reward Perks:
    - 5 Challenger League matches is a literal nothingburger
    - Double Elite League rewards means you double rate at which you get master gladiator costumes to dismantle for 5 shiny coins. 5 shiny coins is worth 200 gems, so I value this at around 400 gems.
    - All slots of equipment storage is massive QOL but not necessary

**If you do decide to buy VIP packages like basic/premium/all-in-one, ONLY buy from the Chief Club for 10% discount + it's also priced lower for some reason**

#### Everything else

- World Boss ($13) and Valiant World Boss ($45) packages are expensive but will allow you to skip all roadblocks. You can jump straight into chaos with a blaze hammer Paladin, then clear the corrupted ranch and Cx1 FB (and beyond) with a glacial bow.
- Memory fragments are extremely hard to come by in early game, so a few set of Daily Paragon's Memory Pack for $3 each can get you to the stage where you can kill Cx1 FB and actually collect a reasonable amount of them daily.

Doing the above two will immediately save 2+ months of grinding for VWB weapons, and even more on memory fragments.

- Chief Club limited costumes: Costume + 600 gems + 10 shiny coins aka 1000 gem value for $4.
  - I don't think this is worth it, but it's maximum gem value per dollar.

**To summarize, you do NOT need to spend any money on this game and things like VIP simply aid QOL. To maximize enjoyment, I'd get ad skip to get ad goblin gems (2700 gems a month), and possibly buy auto-dungeon when I have a 326 or 376 dungeon slave and needed helmets. You could also make a script (at risk of ban) to auto-dungeon for you, but the probability of getting banned is low unless you're running it more than 5+ hours at a time. Making a script to also go through all 15 ad goblin ads (or the entire ad routine) is also trivial if you're hellbent on saving $7.69**
